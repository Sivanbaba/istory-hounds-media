It's NOT four tax returns. A Making Tax Digital quarterly update is just a summary of your income and expenses totals, with no tax to pay each quarter. MTD quarterly updates explained, self employed bookkeeping, sole trader tax UK, HMRC digital records. General info, not tax advice.
