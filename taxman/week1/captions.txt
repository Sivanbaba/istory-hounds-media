1. Day 1 12:30pm · It's turnover, not profit
It's turnover, not profit. Turn over more than £30k and Making Tax Digital starts for you in April 2027, even if you only keep £15k. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #selfemployed #soletrader #hmrc #uktax

2. Day 1 8pm · The 3 start dates
Sole trader or landlord? Three start dates: April 2026 (over £50k), April 2027 (over £30k), April 2028 (over £20k). Which door is yours? General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #soletrader #landlord #selfemployed #uktax

3. Day 2 12:30pm · The new deadlines
Your one yearly tax job becomes five: four quarterly updates (7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb, 7 May, per HMRC's campaign) plus a final declaration by 31 January. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #selfassessment #soletrader #hmrc #uktax

4. Day 2 8pm · What a quarterly update is
It's not four tax returns. A quarterly update is a summary of your totals, with no tax to pay at each one. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #selfemployed #soletrader #hmrc #uktax

5. Day 3 12:30pm · The £200 fine
How the new penalty points work: HMRC says one point per missed quarterly update from April 2027, and a £200 fine at four points. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #hmrc #selfemployed #soletrader #uktax

6. Day 3 8pm · Landlords, your rent counts too
One flat plus a bit of side work? Rent and turnover are added together. £18k rent + £14k work = £32k, over the £30k line. General info, not tax advice.
#landlord #makingtaxdigital #propertyuk #selfemployed #uktax

7. Day 4 12:30pm · Job + side hustle
Day job on PAYE plus a side hustle? Your wages are separate. It's the side's turnover plus any rent that counts. General info, not tax advice.
#sidehustle #makingtaxdigital #selfemployed #paye #uktax

8. Day 4 8pm · Can you keep your spreadsheet?
Yes. A spreadsheet counts as a digital record. You'll just need bridging software to send the totals. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #spreadsheet #soletrader #bookkeeping #uktax

9. Day 5 12:30pm · Who's exempt
If using software genuinely isn't reasonable for you (age, disability, health or location), you can apply to be exempt. It's not automatic. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #hmrc #soletrader #landlord #uktax

10. Day 5 8pm · The January panic
Only about 28% of the first group had signed up by April. Early is calm, late is a queue. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #selfassessment #soletrader #accountant #uktax

11. Day 6 12:30pm · The landlord register
Every landlord in England will register themselves and each property on the new PRS Database. Councils are reporting about £65 per property a year. General info, not legal or tax advice.
#landlord #rentersrightsact #propertyuk #landlordtips #uk

12. Day 6 8pm · Section 21 is gone
"No fault" evictions ended on 1 May 2026 under the Renters' Rights Act. For how-to on evictions, ask a solicitor. General info, not legal advice.
#section21 #landlord #rentersrightsact #propertyuk #uk

13. Day 7 12:30pm · The final declaration
Your Self Assessment becomes a final declaration by 31 January. Little and often wins. General info, not tax advice.
#selfassessment #makingtaxdigital #soletrader #hmrc #uktax

14. Day 7 8pm · Myth: HMRC will tell me
Don't wait for a letter. Whether you're over a threshold is worked out from your own figures. General info, not tax advice.
#makingtaxdigital #hmrc #soletrader #landlord #uktax
